Photosynthesis

What & where?

  • Plants, algae, some bacteria make sugar from CO₂ + H₂O using light
  • light energy → chemical energy (stored in glucose)
  • Happens in chloroplasts — mostly leaf mesophyll cells
  • Chlorophyll absorbs red + blue light, reflects green ∴ leaves look green
Overall equation:

6CO₂ + 6H₂O —light, chlorophyll→ C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂

Common mistake!
"Dark reaction" ≠ happens at night. Calvin cycle needs ATP + NADPH from the light rxn ∴ it runs in daytime.

Stage 1: Light reaction

  • Where: thylakoid membrane
  • Light excites chlorophyll in PS II → PS I
  • Photolysis: 2H₂O → 4H⁺ + 4e⁻ + O₂
  • Makes ATP (via ATP synthase) + NADPH
  • O₂ comes from CO₂ H₂O!

Stage 2: Calvin cycle

  • Where: stroma · light-independent
  • RuBisCO fixes CO₂ onto RuBP (5C) → 2 × 3-PGA (3C)
  • ATP + NADPH reduce 3-PGA → G3P (sugar)
  • Most G3P regenerates RuBP; some → glucose, starch
  • e.g. per 1 CO₂ fixed: 3 ATP + 2 NADPH

Limiting factors

  • Light intensity ↑ → rate ↑ … until it plateaus
  • CO₂ conc. ↑ → rate ↑ … until it plateaus
  • Temperature → enzymes; too hot → enzymes denature, rate ↓
  • Slowest factor sets the rate (law of limiting factors)
