Which one should I add?

Isometric 3D-style

Best for real things you can picture: machines, buildings, data centres, factories, the electricity grid, a city. Feels like an illustration.

Map view

Best for journeys and places: supply chains, travel routes, trade, how a parcel or a request moves. Metro-map style, not exact geography.

Preview only. The days on the map are example timings. Exact country or city outlines are not included — those need real map data.
